--Directions
Simply start the ROM and use the left and right keypad buttons to navigate
through the songs.

In order to play this album properly you'll need specific hardware to burn
the image of the ROM onto a cartridge.  If this hardware isn't available, we
suggest you try to run it through the SMYGB emulator.  Other emulators that
work include NOCASH and VGB, but their sound emulation is of lesser quality.
